🎯 WORDS CLASS DETAILS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. CORRECT
   Sign: Thumb and index finger pointing upward or checkmark motion
   Context: Used to indicate something is correct or right
   
2. NICE
   Sign: Hand motion from chest outward with positive expression
   Context: Expressing appreciation or compliment
   
3. YOU
   Sign: Pointing gesture toward the other person
   Context: Directing attention to the other person
   
4. SORRY
   Sign: Hand over heart or circular motion on chest
   Context: Expressing apology or regret
   
5. WHERE
   Sign: Shrug with hands or repeated questioning gesture
   Context: Asking for location or direction

🔧 MODEL ARCHITECTURE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Input: 224×224 RGB Image
  ↓
MobileNetV2 (Pre-trained on ImageNet)
  ├─ 52 Convolutional layers
  ├─ Inverted residual blocks
  └─ Optimized for mobile/embedded systems
  ↓
Global Average Pooling 2D
  ↓
Dense Layer (128 units, ReLU activation)
  ↓
Dropout (30% rate)
  ↓
Dense Layer (5 units, Softmax activation)
  ↓
Output: Probability distribution over 5 words
